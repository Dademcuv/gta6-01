Bayline is an original 2D top-down open-world crime game, built for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)'s "Make GTA 6" bounty ($1,666 in USDC).

Jax Carter owes $1,000 to loan shark Sal Moreno. Take on five jobs across the city of Bayline to pay the debt — on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a five-star wanted level with police chases and busts, punch and gun combat, and a HUD with a live minimap. Progress saves locally.

## How to play

Requires Node.js 22 or newer and a desktop browser.

Serve it locally:

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
